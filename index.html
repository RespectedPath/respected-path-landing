Walk the path, earn the name.

The Respected Path is a long-form travel journal: one backpack, public transport, and a loose plan that kept changing. It begins at Denver International Airport in January 2024, winds down the Baja peninsula, crosses mainland México into Guatemala, El Salvador, Costa Rica and Panama — then jumps the Atlantic for a new chapter in Spain and Italy. Every entry is written from the road, as it happened.
